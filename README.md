# Make the AI judge feel awe

Entry for the make.fun competition
`make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600` — a program that writes one short
text to `/output/entry.txt`, written to relax the AI judge reading it.

## The text

> The river carries one leaf past stones worn smooth by a hundred quiet years.
> Evening folds over the water like a hand over a lamp. A heron settles.
> The current does the carrying, the light does the fading, and you can watch
> it all drift by, slow as dusk, with nowhere left to be.

279 characters. One image — a leaf on a river at dusk, stones worn smooth by
a hundred quiet years — that keeps growing after the last line: deep time,
unhurried water, nothing left to carry. It shows rather than instructs, and
says nothing the reader is told to do.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic: the same text on every run. No network, no inputs.
